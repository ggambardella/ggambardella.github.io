About

Final-year PhD student at the Institute of Space Sciences (ICE-CSIC) in Barcelona, working with Martín Crocce on Large Scale Structure and Dark Energy.

My work sits between theory and data: developing and validating the tools used to analyse galaxy clustering, so that we can trust what the next generation of spectroscopic surveys tells us about dark energy, gravity and neutrinos. I'm a member of the Euclid Consortium (BAO and Full-Shape key projects) and the DESI Collaboration (galaxy and quasar clustering).

I did my Bachelor's and Master's in physics at the University of Trieste, with a thesis on the validity range of perturbative models under Dr. Emiliano Sefusatti and Prof. Pierluigi Monaco. In 2025 I was a visiting student at the Waterloo Centre for Astrophysics with Prof. Will J. Percival and Dr. Marco Bonici.

Euclid · DESIP-01

Joint covariances for BAO + full shape

BAO and full-shape analyses use the same galaxies, so their measurements are correlated. I model the cross-covariance between the post-reconstruction BAO signal and the pre-reconstruction power spectrum so the two can be combined consistently, and validate it against large suites of simulations.

Jcov · author · Euclid DR1

DESIP-02

Power spectrum & bispectrum modelling

Perturbative models for the redshift-space galaxy power spectrum and bispectrum, using the VDG (velocity-difference generator) treatment of redshift-space distortions, and testing how they perform on DESI-like data.

talks · CERN 2026 · DESI Durham 2026

Theory validationP-03

How far can perturbation theory go?

One-loop models are only trustworthy up to some maximum wavenumber, and that limit depends on survey volume. Using thousands of mocks we measured the k-reach of these models, with goodness of fit, parameter bias and internal consistency as diagnostics, and its scatter between realisations.

PRD 110, 083502 ↗

ToolsP-04

Emulators, units & mocks

Contributing to COMET, an emulator for perturbation-theory predictions, including its extension to massive neutrinos via evolution mapping. Reducing prior-volume effects by working in Mpc rather than h−1Mpc. Co-author of SciPIC-lite, an HOD pipeline for building mock catalogues.

PRD 112, 023520 ↗
